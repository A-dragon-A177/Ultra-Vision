## Collect the week @vision [approval]
Ask the founder for this week's raw facts in one message if they were not in the request: what shipped,
content posted and how it did, leads and sales, money in and out, what got stuck. If they are in the
request, summarise them cleanly instead.

## Numbers check @zola
From the facts collected, work out the week's key numbers and how they compare with the 90-day goals.
Show the maths. List the figures that are missing.

## Next week's content @friday
Propose next week's content plan: the pieces, the day for each, and the one idea behind each.

## Plan for next week @vision
Combine everything into next week's plan: three priorities, what each team member takes, and what the
founder must personally do. Under 200 words.
