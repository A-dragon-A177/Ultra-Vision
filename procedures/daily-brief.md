## Morning brief @vision
Read brain/wiki/about-me.md and brain/wiki/index.md first.
Write the founder's morning brief:
- Today's date and a one-line energy read (what kind of day this is for the startup)
- The three things that matter most today, in order. For each: the action to take first.
- Suggested team handoffs: one specific task to give to each of two team members (Friday, Jocasta, Ultron, etc.)
- One thing the founder should NOT work on today (protects focus)
- One quick win: a task under 15 minutes that moves something forward

Keep it tight — under 180 words. Skip what isn't relevant.
If the company profile and brain are still empty, ask the founder to fill them in first.
